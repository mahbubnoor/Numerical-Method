Q2 When do you use the backward formula instead of the forward one?
When xxx lies near the end of the table: the backward formula uses differences at the end (∇kyn\nabla^k y_n∇kyn​) and keeps ∣p∣<1|p| < 1∣p∣<1, so fewer terms are needed and the error is smaller.

Q4 Why is extrapolation dangerous?
The error term contains p(p−1)⋯(p−n)p(p-1)\cdots(p-n)p(p−1)⋯(p−n), which grows rapidly when xxx is outside the table; nothing in the data controls the polynomial there.
