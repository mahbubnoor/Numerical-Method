Q4 What is the difference between local and global truncation error?
Local: the error made in a single step starting from the exact value. Global: the total error at a given xxx after all steps. With n∝1/hn \propto 1/hn∝1/h steps, a local error O(hp+1)O(h^{p+1})O(hp+1) accumulates to a global error O(hp)O(h^p)O(hp).
