Q3 Distinguish an ill-conditioned problem and an unstable algorithm, with one example each.
Ill-conditioned: exact answer very sensitive to data; e.g. x+y=2x + y = 2x+y=2, x+1.0001y=2.0001x + 1.0001y = 2.0001x+1.0001y=2.0001 (κ≈4×104\kappa\approx4\times10^4κ≈4×104).
