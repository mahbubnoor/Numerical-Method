Q2 Why is LU better than Gaussian elimination when there are many right-hand sides?
Elimination costs 23n3\tfrac23 n^332​n3 and depends only on AAA; LU stores its result (L and U). Each further b\mathbf{b}b needs only forward and back substitution, 2n22n^22n2 operations, instead of repeating the 23n3\tfrac23 n^332​n3.
Q3 When can Cholesky be used, and what does it cost?
When AAA is symmetric positive definite. A=LLTA = LL^TA=LLT costs about 13n3\tfrac13 n^331​n3 operations (half of LU) and needs no pivoting. If a number under a square root is ≤0\le 0≤0, AAA is not positive definite.
