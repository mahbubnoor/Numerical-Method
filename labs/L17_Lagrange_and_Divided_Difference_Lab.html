Q2 Give one advantage of divided differences over Lagrange.
Adding a new point only adds one row to the table and one term to the formula; Lagrange must recompute every basis polynomial.

Q4 What is inverse interpolation?
Finding the xxx that gives a known yyy by interpolating xxx as a function of yyy (swap the columns of the table). It works when yyy is monotonic over the data.
Q5 Why not find the coefficients by solving the Vandermonde system?
It costs O(n3)O(n^3)O(n3) and the Vandermonde matrix is badly ill-conditioned (about 101610^{16}1016 for 20 equally spaced points on [0,1][0, 1][0,1]), so the coefficients lose all their digits.
