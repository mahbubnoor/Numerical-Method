Q1 What is the Runge phenomenon?
Interpolating a smooth function by one polynomial through many equally spaced points can give large oscillations near the ends of the interval, and the error grows as more points are added (e.g. 1/(1+25x2)1/(1+25x^2)1/(1+25x2): error 1.92 with 11 points, 60 with 21).

Q4 Why are splines preferred to a single high-degree polynomial for long data sets?
Each piece is low degree and depends mainly on nearby data, so there are no wild end oscillations; the error falls steadily (like h4h^4h4) as points are added; and the setup is a tridiagonal system, O(n)O(n)O(n) work.
Q5 Give two situations where a cubic spline is a poor choice.
Data with jumps or sharp corners (overshoot); noisy measurements (the spline passes through the noise; use least squares); also extrapolation beyond the data.
