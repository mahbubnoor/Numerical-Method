Q4 Why is Cramer's rule not used for a system of 20 equations?
It needs 21 determinants of size 20; by cofactor expansion that is about 1.4×10201.4\times10^{20}1.4×1020 operations (about 4400 years at 10910^9109 per second). Gaussian elimination needs about 23203≈5900\tfrac23 20^3 \approx 590032​203≈5900 operations.
