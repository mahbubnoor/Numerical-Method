Q3 Why is numerical differentiation called ill-conditioned?
It divides small differences of data by small steps: a data error δ\deltaδ produces a derivative error of about δ/h\delta/hδ/h (first derivative) or 4δ/h24\delta/h^24δ/h2 (second). Smaller hhh reduces truncation error but magnifies noise and round-off.
