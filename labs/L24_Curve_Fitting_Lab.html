Q4 Why is fitting ln⁡y\ln ylny not the same as fitting yyy?
It minimises the squared errors of ln⁡y\ln ylny, i.e. roughly relative errors, so small values of yyy get more weight; the result differs slightly from minimising ∑(yi−aebxi)2\sum (y_i - ae^{bx_i})^2∑(yi​−aebxi​)2.
Q5 What is overfitting and how is it detected?
A model with too many parameters fits the noise of the training data: training error is tiny but error on new data is large. It is detected by measuring the error on data not used for fitting (validation or test data).
