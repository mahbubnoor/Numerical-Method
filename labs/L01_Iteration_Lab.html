Q3 Distinguish analytical and numerical solutions with one example of each.
Analytical: exact formula, e.g. x=(−b±b2−4ac)/2ax = (-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac})/2ax=(−b±b2−4ac​)/2a. Numerical: approximate number from an algorithm with an error estimate, e.g. x=cos⁡x⇒x≈0.739085x = \cos x \Rightarrow x \approx 0.739085x=cosx⇒x≈0.739085 by iteration.
Q4 Why is a maximum number of iterations needed even when we have a tolerance?
If the method diverges or oscillates, or if the tolerance is smaller than the precision of the computer allows (about 10−1610^{-16}10−16 relative), the tolerance is never met and the loop would run forever.
