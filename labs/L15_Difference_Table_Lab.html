Difference Table Lab

Build difference tables for your own data, read off the degree, and find errors and missing values.

Type equally spaced data. Switch between the forward diagonal (Δ, used near the start) and the backward diagonal (∇, used near the end).

Add an error ε to one entry and watch how it spreads. Then hide it: can a classmate find which entry you changed?
