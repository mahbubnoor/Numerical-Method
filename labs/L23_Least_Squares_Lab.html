Q1 What is the principle of least squares?
Choose the parameters of the model so that the sum of the squares of the residuals S=∑(yi−y^i)2S = \sum (y_i - \hat y_i)^2S=∑(yi​−y^​i​)2 is as small as possible.
Q2 Write the normal equations for fitting y=a+bxy = a + bxy=a+bx.
na+b∑x=∑yna + b\sum x = \sum yna+b∑x=∑y and a∑x+b∑x2=∑xya\sum x + b\sum x^2 = \sum xya∑x+b∑x2=∑xy.
Q3 How does least squares differ from interpolation?
Interpolation passes through every data point (as many coefficients as points); least squares uses fewer coefficients than points and passes close to them, smoothing the noise.
Q4 What does R2=0.95R^2 = 0.95R2=0.95 mean?
95% of the variation of yyy about its mean is explained by the model; it does not prove that the model is correct, so look at the residuals too.
Q5 Why can solving the normal equations be inaccurate?
cond(ATAA^{T}AATA) = cond(AAA)², so nearly dependent columns (e.g. x = years) lose about twice as many digits; centre the data or use QR.
