Q4 Distinguish round-off error and truncation error with one example each.
Round-off: from finite digits, e.g. 1/3→0.33331/3 \to 0.33331/3→0.3333. Truncation: from stopping an infinite process, e.g. ex≈1+x+x2/2e^x \approx 1 + x + x^2/2ex≈1+x+x2/2; the neglected terms are the truncation error.
