Q3 Distinguish the secant method from false position.
Both use the chord formula. False position keeps the pair of points that brackets the root (always converges, may stall); secant always uses the two latest points (no bracket, faster, may diverge).

Q5 Which method would you choose, and why: (a) fff is a noisy measurement; (b) fff is a polynomial with an easy derivative and a good guess is known; (c) fff comes from a slow simulation with no derivative?
(a) Bisection: needs only signs, robust to noise. (b) Newton: quadratic, f′f'f′ cheap. (c) Secant (or Brent): no derivative, one evaluation per step, superlinear.
